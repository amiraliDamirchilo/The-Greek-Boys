A True Taste of Greece on the Ajman Corniche.

At The Greek Boys, we bring the vibrant street food culture and comforting flavors of Greece right to the UAE. Located conveniently at Faizan Tower on the Ajman Corniche, every dish is prepared with fresh ingredients, genuine feta cheese, aromatic Greek oregano, and homemade tzatziki sauces.

Marinated chicken skewers, beef souvlakia, and slow-roasted gyros.
